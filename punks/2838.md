In 2018 ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe acquired 4 CryptoPunks for their permanent collection. Punks 2838 & [5449](https://burnedpunks.com/punks/5449) were accidentally sent to the punks contract address by ZKM employee Daniel Heiss as a result of copying the wrong address while attempting to transfer them. In a 2022 interview with [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/01/21/a-german-museum-has-accidentally-lost-access-to-two-highly-valuable-nfts), Heiss reframed the error into “a statement about what blockchain” and asking “maybe \[ownership\] is less about being able to trade the work, and more about establishing a chain of provenance.” In 2025 these two burned punks were displayed at ZKM in their *T* [*he Story That Never Ends*](https://zkm.de/en/2025/04/the-story-that-never-ends) exhibition alongside punks 1286 & 2554 which remain part of the institutions collection - wall text noting the burn and discussing what that means for digital ownership and permanence.

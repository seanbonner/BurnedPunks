Punk 3493 was burned accidentally during an attempted move on April 19, 2019 becoming the first punk ever to be burned a few months shy of the 2 year mint anniversary. The owner, who originally claimed 15 zombies, was consolidating several wallets and over the course of a day had already transferred 67 punks, and accidentally hit send on this transfer without putting in a recipient address into the form. On Discord at the time he said he had no explanation for the “1” at the end of the address, it was not typed manually and could have just been an artifact of an incorrect copy & paste or an unexpected default autofill.

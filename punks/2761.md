Punk 2761 was burned accidentally on December 17, 2021 due to pasting a malformed wallet address in the transfer field. At the time the owner stated in the Cryptopunks Discord that they had been attempting to send the punk to [this account](https://cryptopunks.app/cryptopunks/accountinfo?account=0x069ad4294f8b920eb96c57278b0468cd10d951a6) but accidentally dropped the 0 in the front and somehow added a 6 on the end. While the recipient isn’t a standard burn address, it’s a random address and the accidental result is similar to what [Dmitri Cherniak intentionally did with Dead Ringers](https://deadringers.dmitricherniak.com) as the chance of someone ever generating the seed to this wallet is 16 to the power of 40.
